# Varnik Labs: brand film (45 s, 1080p)

`output/varnik-labs-brand-film.mp4`: a 45-second marketing film built from the
sales deck and the brand identity book. It follows the deck's story:
**Lights off → Switch on → Lights everywhere → Your turn.**

Everything is generated by code: the edit, the motion graphics, the music and
the sound design. The photography comes from the brand book.

## Brand compliance

- Palette: Royal Purple `#6D28D9` (dominant), Electric Lime `#D4F55A` (one lime word per line), Midnight, Ink, Lavender, Cloud
- Type: Sora ExtraBold headlines, DM Sans body and eyebrows (from Google Fonts)
- No lime text on white; lime only on purple, Ink or Midnight
- Logo animated per the brand's motion page (fly in, snap, say the name, shine), using the official logo cut from the brand book rather than a redraw
- Logo is never stretched, tilted at rest, recoloured outside the palette or given effects at rest

## Rebuild

```bash
pip install numpy scipy opencv-python-headless pillow   # plus ffmpeg + poppler-utils
./render.sh path/to/Varnik_Labs_Brand_Identity.pdf
```

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `timeline.py` | Scene timings, palette and the SFX cue sheet, shared by picture and sound so every hit lands on its cut |
| `prepare.py` | Extracts photos from the PDF, downloads the fonts, cuts the logo into animatable pieces |
| `video.py` | Renders the picture. `--stills 1.0 22.6` writes preview frames |
| `audio.py` | Synthesizes the score and sound design (no samples, so there are no licensing issues) |
| `render.sh` | Runs everything and muxes `output/varnik-labs-brand-film.mp4` |

To retime the edit, change `SCENES` / `CUES` in `timeline.py` and re-run.
