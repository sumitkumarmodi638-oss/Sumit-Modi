## Instagram Reels: which signals matter, and what Mosseri/Meta have said officially (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, said in January 2025 that the three most important ranking signals are **watch time, likes and sends (DM shares)**. Sends carry more weight for reaching non-followers ("unconnected" content), and likes carry more weight with existing followers. Meta's Transparency Center Reels card centres on predicted watch behaviour. In 2025–2026 Instagram also added Trial Reels (test with non-followers first), 3-minute Reels, and steadily tougher originality rules. Since 30 April 2026, accounts that mostly repost are excluded from recommendations.

### Cited Findings
**Official ranking signals**
- Mosseri (January 2025 video series): "the top three signals for ranking are watch time, likes and sends". He told creators to track **average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach** in Insights — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-reveals-key-factors-for-boosting-reach-8646916)
- Mosseri: "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." In practice, DM shares drive reach to non-followers — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- A widely repeated claim says sends carry "3–5x the weight of likes" outside the follower base. It comes from CreatorFlow data quoted by marketing blogs, not from Meta, so it is **unverified** — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)
- Meta Transparency Center ("Instagram Reels Chaining" system card): the pipeline gathers candidate Reels, applies input signals, makes predictions, then ranks. The listed predictions include how likely a user is to **watch more of a reel than comparable reels**, to open a tall Explore reel full screen, and to **use the reel's audio**. Only excerpts were visible, and no explicit share prediction appeared in them — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-reels-chaining/)
- Third-party guides describe Reels predictions as focused on likelihood to reshare and to watch through. Instagram also surveys users on whether a reel was "worth watching" — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-algorithm/); [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm/)
- Blogs often cite "60% completion" as a target. This is a rule of thumb from marketing blogs, **not an Instagram disclosure** — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)
- A December 2025 year-end memo from Mosseri reportedly said Instagram would favour raw, real, human-made content over AI-generated material in 2026. This was reported by one marketing source and is **not independently confirmed** — [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/instagram-influencer-marketing/)

**Trial Reels**
- Trial Reels let professional accounts show a Reel to **non-followers first**. After about 24 hours the creator sees views, likes, comments and shares, then chooses to share it with followers or archive it. Mosseri said the aim was to reduce the pressure of posting — [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-new-trial-reels/)
- The feature was tested with selected creators for almost a year before it rolled out to all professional accounts — [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/how-to-test-reels-with-non-followers-on-instagram/)
- One third-party guide says Trial Reels launched in late 2024 and reached all creators with 1,000+ followers by mid-2025. This guide is unofficial, and its eligibility details are unverified — [Postfast](https://postfa.st/blog/instagram-trial-reels)

**3-minute Reels**
- On 18 January 2025 Mosseri announced that Reels can be up to **3 minutes** long, up from 90 seconds. He said Instagram was still focused on short-form — [David Zucker Substack](https://davidzucker.substack.com/p/three-minute-instagram-reels-have); [t3n](https://t3n.de/news/?p=1675223)
- NetInfluencer reported that Instagram would give 3-minute Reels equal recommendation treatment — [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-give-equal-recommendation-to-reels-3-minutes-in-length/)
- **Conflicting claim:** Inro (updated July 2026), citing a Socialinsider analysis, says distribution to non-followers falls away at around 180 seconds. This is not consistent with the official announcement — [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-reels-length)

**Originality rules and penalties for reposting**
- On **30 April 2026** Instagram extended its originality rules beyond Reels. Accounts that mostly post unoriginal photos or carousels no longer appear in recommendation surfaces (Feed recommendations and Explore/Discover). Followers still see reposts from accounts they already follow — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=192084); [MediaPost](https://mediapost.com/publications/article/414773/instagram-algorithm-update-discourages-reposted-co.html)
- What counts as original: cropping, resizing, adding a watermark or adding a credit tag does **not** make content original. Adding your own commentary, analysis, context or original graphics can — [Sociallyin](https://sociallyin.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-update-repost/); [Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/instagram-updates-its-original-content-policy)
- CTI Digital reports a specific threshold: accounts that post more than **10 reposted Reels in 30 days** are penalised. Other sources do not repeat this number, so it is **unconfirmed** — [CTI Digital](https://ctidigital.com/insights/instagram-update-impact-on-re-posting-and-user-generated-content/)

### Inferences
- The metrics to optimise on Instagram are **sends per reach** (to reach non-followers), **average watch time and retention** (strong hook, loopable endings), and likes per reach (for the follower base).
- Agencies should design Reels that people want to DM to a colleague or friend: checklists, "tag the person who…" relatable moments, local or India-specific insights.
- Use Trial Reels to A/B test hooks with non-followers before publishing to the main feed.
- Never repost watermarked TikTok or other-platform files, and avoid pure clip-aggregation. Remix only with added commentary.
- 3-minute Reels are allowed, but evidence on how far long Reels reach non-followers conflicts. Default to under 60–90 seconds unless the content holds retention.

### Gaps
- Meta's full Transparency Center text could not be fetched, so it is unconfirmed whether the card names "sends/shares" explicitly.
- No official Instagram numbers exist for the "3-second hold" or for a completion or replay threshold. All such figures come from third parties.
- The exact wording of Mosseri's 2026 statements, including the December 2025 memo, was not verified from primary posts.

## YouTube Shorts: viewed vs swiped away, how views are counted (2025 change), retention benchmarks, and the Shorts-to-long-form funnel

### Takeaway
Since **31 March 2025**, a Shorts "view" counts every start or replay, with no minimum watch time. The old metric continues as **"engaged views"**, and monetisation and YPP eligibility still use engaged views. YouTube publishes no official "viewed vs swiped away" target. Creator-industry heuristics put the goal at about **70–80% viewed (stayed to watch)**. 2026 data suggests a few Shorts a month correlate with higher long-form views, while very high Shorts volume does not help.

### Cited Findings
- From 31 March 2025 a Shorts view is counted each time a Short starts playing or replays, with no minimum watch time. The previous count, which required a minimum number of seconds watched, is now called "**engaged views**" and appears in YouTube Analytics Advanced Mode — [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/404562/youtube-changes-how-it-counts-shorts-views.html); [MobileSyrup](https://mobilesyrup.com/2025/03/27/youtube-changes-how-it-counts-shorts-views/)
- YPP eligibility and earnings stay based on engaged views. YouTube advises using engaged views to compare content published before and after 31 March 2025 — [Search Engine Journal](https://beta.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-changes-shorts-view-counts-no-change-to-monetization/543005/); [Movavi](https://movavi.com/news/youtube-revamps-shorts-metrics-what-creators-need-to-know)
- One Indian outlet reports a later change that extends instant view counting to long-form videos from 24 August, with engaged views kept in Advanced Mode. This is **unverified** by other sources — [t2online](https://t2online.in/tech/tech-news/youtube-changes-how-views-are-counted--and-the-numbers-are-about-to-climb-faster/2007332)
- "Viewed vs swiped away" (now labelled "**Stayed to watch**") is the share of feed impressions where the viewer did not swipe away. YouTube does not publish a universal seconds threshold for it — [Creator Essentials glossary](https://www.creatoressentials.com/glossary/viewed-vs-swiped-away/)
- There is **no official universal target**. Creator-tool sites suggest aiming for **75–80% viewed and 20–25% swiped away** and benchmarking against your own Shorts of similar length and topic. This is a heuristic — [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/p/youtube-shorts-analytics-metrics-viral); [Bytecap research](https://www.bytecap.io/research/youtube-shorts-retention-benchmarks)
- **Metricool 2026 YouTube Study** (799,718 videos from 71,177 accounts, comparing February 2025 with February 2026):
  - The Shorts feed supplied **61% of measured organic views**.
  - Average Shorts view duration fell about 67%, to roughly **16 seconds**.
  - Long-form views rose 76% year over year, but average long-form view duration fell from 3.98 to 2.51 minutes — [Metricool press release](https://metricool.com/press-release-youtube-study-2026/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/press-release/20260727-metricools-2026-youtube-study-reveals-a-slow-burn-effect-more-views-less/)
- Funnel effect in the same Metricool study:
  - Channels posting about **5 Shorts a month** averaged about **8,789 long-form views per video**, against 4,364 for channels posting no Shorts. This is a correlation, not proof of cause.
  - Channels posting **35 or more Shorts a month** fell back to the long-form view levels of channels posting none — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/03/youtube-shorts-long-form-study-metricool/amp/); [Metricool trends](https://metricool.com/youtube-trends/)
- In an Adobe Express survey (June 2026, 507 creators with at least 1,000 subscribers), 49% said Shorts **under 30 seconds** are their most effective top-of-funnel tool for driving clicks to long-form. This is self-reported — [Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/youtube-shorts-length-study)
- AIR Media-Tech's analysis of about 18,000 channels finds the funnel benefit is mixed and depends on niche. For example, gaming Shorts viewers often do not convert to long-form — [AIR](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/do-youtube-shorts-help-your-long-form-videos-grow-data-from-18000-channels)
- YouTube's Todd Beaupré reportedly said creators focus too much on the first hours after upload. The source is a secondhand summary — [AITuber](https://aituber.app/blog/youtube-shorts-not-getting-views/)

### Inferences
- Since the change, raw Shorts view counts are inflated. Agencies should report **engaged views** and **stayed-to-watch %** to clients, and should not compare pre- and post-March 2025 raw views.
- Practical targets: 70–80%+ stayed to watch, with average view duration close to or above video length (replays) for Shorts under 30 seconds.
- For a B2B or agency channel, aim for a moderate cadence of a few Shorts per week (about 5–20 a month), each linking to a related long-form video. Do not flood the channel.

### Gaps
- No official YouTube retention or "stayed to watch" benchmarks were found, and none by industry, B2B or account size.
- No India-specific Shorts benchmarks were found.
- Shorts engagement rate (likes and comments per view) by industry for 2025–2026 was not found in a reliable report.

## LinkedIn: vertical video feed, video reach vs other formats, dwell time, B2B results, company page vs personal profile

### Takeaway
LinkedIn expanded its vertical, TikTok-style video feed and creator video tools in February 2025, reporting video viewership up **36% year over year**. Socialinsider's business-page data shows video engagement around **5.9–6.0%**, above the platform average of 5.2%. Video **views fell 36% year over year**, however, and native documents (carousels) still lead on engagement. Personal profiles earn higher engagement rates than company pages, while impressions per post are roughly similar. Dwell time is widely described as a core signal, but LinkedIn has published no numeric thresholds.

### Cited Findings
**Vertical video feed and video growth**
- In February 2025 LinkedIn added vertical-video features: in-feed design updates, better video search, and analytics such as **average watch time**. A desktop version of the vertical feed was new, and the mobile version came first. LinkedIn first tested the feed in March 2024 — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/04/linkedin-amps-up-vertical-video-tools-as-uploads-jump-36); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/02/04/linkedin-video-update-tiktok-alternative)
- Axios (4 February 2025): users were watching video at a rate **36% higher than the year before** — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/02/04/linkedin-video-update-tiktok-alternative)
- A summary of LinkedIn Creative Labs research reports that video viewership rose 36% in 2024 to **154 billion views** — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/linkedin-creative-labs-reveals-what-makes-b2b-video-content-successful/)
- Videos cannot be posted directly to the immersive video feed. It pulls from feed video uploads. The video tab sits in the mobile bottom navigation — [Kapwing](https://kapwing.com/resources/how-to-post-videos-on-linkedin-in-2025-2)

**B2B video findings from LinkedIn**
- LinkedIn Creative Labs studied more than 13,000 B2B video **ads** and found:
  - **73% of video completions** and 49% of engagement rates depend on creative decisions rather than budget or production value.
  - Brand films of **31–60 seconds** delivered a 61% engagement uplift over 6-second formats.
  - These findings are about paid ads, not organic posts — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/linkedin-creative-labs-reveals-what-makes-b2b-video-content-successful/)
- LinkedIn's B2B Marketing Benchmark Report 2024 found that 58% of B2B marketers said short-form content delivered their highest ROI. This is 2024 data — [onlinemarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/36-prozent-mehr-linkedin-video-boom-neue-features)

**Benchmarks (Socialinsider 2026 LinkedIn report, 1.3M business posts, data January–December 2025 plus 2026 quarterly updates)**

| Format / measure | Engagement rate |
|---|---|
| Video, 2025 | **6.00%** (5.60% in 2024; up 7% year over year) |
| Video, Q1 and Q2 2026 | **5.90%** in both quarters |
| Native documents (carousels), 2025 | **7.00%** |
| Platform average, 2026 | **5.20%** (up 8% year over year) |

- The same report notes video **views down 36% year over year**, with the largest pages still generating the most views — [Socialinsider LinkedIn benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/linkedin-benchmark/); [PDF](https://cdn.socialinsider.io/documents/linkedin_benchmarks_2026.pdf)

**Dwell time and format reach**
- Dwell time is widely described as a major ranking signal in 2025–2026, measured from when a post enters the viewport until the reader scrolls past. A "6+ second" threshold attributed to a "LinkedIn Creator Report 2026" could not be verified — [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com/blog/linkedin-algorithm)
- Carousel reach advantage over text reportedly fell from 11.2x (Q1 2025) to 3.7x (Q2 2025). Video reach claims are mixed, from a "200% drop" to "5x engagement", and none has a clear primary source — [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/linkedin-carousel-vs-text-post); [SEO Works](https://www.seoworks.co.uk/downloads/data-led-analysis-of-linkedin-video-vs-carousels/)
- Videos **under 2 minutes** had the highest engagement rate of the length buckets in one analysis. Videos under 3 minutes got slightly higher impressions — [SEO Works](https://www.seoworks.co.uk/downloads/data-led-analysis-of-linkedin-video-vs-carousels/)

**Company page vs personal profile**
- **Metricool 2026 LinkedIn study:**
  - Personal profiles reach a **2.60% engagement rate**, against **1.60% for company pages**.
  - Impressions per post are roughly equal: company pages about 832, personal profiles about 818.
  - Company pages get more shares per post: 1.16 against 0.08 — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/linkedin-trends/); [blck alpaca](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/linkedin-company-page-or-personal-profile-reach)
- Richard van der Blom's Algorithm Insights Report 2025 is cited secondhand as putting **company page organic reach at about 1–2%**, with page posts making up about 2% of what users see in feed. **Not verified** against the original report — [blck alpaca](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/linkedin-company-page-or-personal-profile-reach)
- An Ordinal analysis finds company pages take the external-link reach penalty, while personal profiles show almost none. This is single-vendor data — [Ordinal](https://www.tryordinal.com/blog/linkedin-link-penalty-study)

### Inferences
- A small agency should put its LinkedIn video through **founder and employee personal profiles**, which earn higher engagement, and re-share from the company page.
- Use 30–90 second vertical or square native video with burned-in captions and a hook that holds dwell time. Alternate with document carousels, which still lead on engagement.
- Measure success by engagement rate (target about 5–6% on business-page video posts per Socialinsider) and average watch time. Do not judge by raw views, which are falling platform-wide.

### Gaps
- LinkedIn has published no official ranking-signal documentation for the video feed, and no dwell-time thresholds were found.
- No completion or view-through benchmarks for **organic** LinkedIn video were found; the Creative Labs data covers ads.
- The van der Blom 2025 and 2026 reports could not be accessed directly.

## Benchmarks: engagement, reach, completion and share rates by account size and industry

### Takeaway
Engagement benchmarks vary widely with the formula used: per follower, per reach, or per view. Business-account Reels engagement by followers is about **0.5%**, per Socialinsider. Reels reach rate per follower falls from about **9.8% for 1K–5K accounts to about 5% for 100K–1M accounts**. The median Instagram engagement across industries, per Rival IQ, is about **0.30–0.36% by followers**. Reliable share-rate and completion benchmarks by industry are scarce.

### Cited Findings
**Instagram Reels engagement rate (Socialinsider, by followers)**
- Reels engagement was about **0.48% in Q2 2026**, down from 0.50% in Q1, with carousels at 0.50% — [Socialinsider Instagram benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/instagram-benchmarks); [ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/blog/instagram-statistics)
- Other secondary sources cite Reels at 0.52% and carousels at 0.55% for 2025 data. A Colorlib roundup attributes a 1.23% Reels figure to Socialinsider, which **conflicts** with Socialinsider's own page — [Colorlib](https://colorlib.com/wp/instagram-engagement-rate/); [inmagic.ai](https://inmagic.ai/instagram-reels-statistics/)

**Instagram Reels reach rate by follower size (reach ÷ followers; Socialinsider, 140,000 business Reels, January–June 2026)**

| Followers | Reels reach rate |
|---|---|
| 1K–5K | **9.78%** |
| 5K–10K | 7.55% |
| 10K–50K | 7.10% |
| 50K–100K | 5.60% |
| 100K–1M | 5.00% |

- In the 1K–5K tier, Reels (9.78%) beat carousels (8.80%) and images (7%) on reach — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/Instagram-reels-stats); [inmagic.ai](https://inmagic.ai/instagram-engagement-rate-benchmarks/)

**Skip rate and share rate**
- Reels skip rate, per inmagic's summary of Socialinsider data, runs from about **65.5% (1K–5K accounts) to 60.5% (100K–1M)**. This implies only about 35–40% of viewers get past the opening — [inmagic.ai](https://inmagic.ai/instagram-engagement-rate-benchmarks/)
- A Reels share-rate benchmark of about 0.5% (average) appears on OwlClaw, labelled "Updated: 2025". Its reach data conflicts with Socialinsider, so it is **low reliability** — [OwlClaw](https://owlclaw.com/benchmarks/instagram-reels-benchmarks)

**Other engagement figures**
- Rival IQ (now part of Quid): the 2026 cross-industry Instagram median is reported at **0.30% per post by followers**, and the 2025 median at 0.36%. Industry spread runs from about 0.14% (Health & Beauty) to 2.10% (Higher Education). These figures come from secondary summaries — [Apaya](https://apaya.com/blog/social-media-benchmarks-instagram); [inmagic.ai](https://inmagic.ai/instagram-engagement-rate-benchmarks/)
- Reels engagement figures of 1.48% (attributed to Hootsuite) and 2.08% also circulate. They **conflict** because they use different denominators, likely reach or views — [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/instagram-reel-statistics/)
- Engagement measured by reach or views produces much higher numbers than engagement by followers. The same industry can show 0.26% in one source and 3.80% in another — [Apaya](https://apaya.com/blog/social-media-benchmarks); [PostPlanify](https://postplanify.com/blog/social-media-engagement-rate-benchmarks-2026)

**LinkedIn and YouTube**
- LinkedIn business-post video engagement is about 5.9–6.0%, and company pages average 1.6% against 2.6% for personal profiles in Metricool's data. See the LinkedIn section.
- Average YouTube Shorts view duration is about 16 seconds (Metricool 2026). See the YouTube section.

### Inferences
Suggested targets for a small agency account with 1K–10K followers. Make sure everyone uses the same formula.

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Reels reach | 8–10% of followers per Reel as baseline; viral outliers reach multiples of follower count via non-followers |
| Reels engagement | 0.5% by followers as average, 1%+ as good; 3–5%+ is a typical "good" range when measured by reach (directional) |
| Hold rate | Beat the about 35–40% that Socialinsider skip rates imply; aim for 50%+ of viewers past 3 seconds |
| Sends/shares | Track sends per reach monthly; no reliable public benchmark exists, so compare against your own median |
| LinkedIn video engagement | About 5–6% on page video (Socialinsider formula) |
| YouTube Shorts | 70–80% stayed to watch |

### Gaps
- No reliable 2025–2026 benchmark covers **agencies, B2B or professional services** specifically for Reels or Shorts. Rival IQ's industry tables could not be accessed.
- No dependable public benchmarks for **completion/view-through rate** or **share/send rate** were found for Reels or Shorts.
- Emplifi, Sprout Social and Hootsuite 2025–2026 report figures could not be retrieved directly.

## Best posting frequency and times, and India-specific data

### Takeaway
Global data points to mid-week, with midday and evening slots, as best for Instagram. Buffer's 2026 analysis of 9.6M posts ranks Thursday 9am, Wednesday 12pm and Wednesday 6pm top, and evenings strongest overall. No large-sample, India-specific (IST) dataset was found. For YouTube, a moderate Shorts cadence (about 5 a month or more, but under 35) correlates best with long-form growth.

### Cited Findings
**Posting times (global data)**
- **Buffer, July 2026** (9.6M Instagram posts, global, all formats): best times are **Thursday 9am, Wednesday 12pm and Wednesday 6pm**. The 6–11pm window produces the strongest engagement on every weekday except Thursday — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram)
- **Later** (more than 6M posts) normalises by engagement rate, which pushes its recommendation toward early morning. Studies that count total engagement favour midday and evening, while studies that normalise for reach favour low-competition hours — [SocialPilot summary](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/best-time-to-post-reels-on-instagram)
- **SocialPilot** (more than 250,000 Reels): **8am and 12pm, Monday–Thursday** (global trends) — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/best-time-to-post-reels-on-instagram)
- Later 2026 is quoted as saying Tuesday and Wednesday 10am–1pm gives the highest reach. The timezone is not stated and the data is not India-specific — [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/instagram-reel-statistics/)

**India-specific**
- A best-time page for India suggests **5:30pm, 6:30pm and 7:30pm IST**. It is based on an editorial baseline, not a large dataset — [timetopost.co](https://timetopost.co/best-time-to-post/instagram/india/)
- India is Instagram's largest market, with about **472.6M users** against 179.9M in the US. YouTube Shorts is described as Reels' main competitor in India — [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/instagram-reel-statistics/)

**Frequency**
- YouTube: about 5 Shorts a month correlates with roughly double the long-form views of channels posting no Shorts. Channels posting 35 or more Shorts a month see no benefit — [Tubefilter/Metricool 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/03/youtube-shorts-long-form-study-metricool/amp/)
- Instagram: no 2025–2026 primary benchmark on ideal Reels frequency was retrieved. Trial Reels let accounts test frequently without spamming followers — [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-new-trial-reels/)

### Inferences
- For Indian audiences, start testing **weekday evenings (about 6–9pm IST)** and **lunchtime (12–2pm IST)**, Tuesday to Thursday. Validate against Instagram Insights "most active times" after 4–6 weeks.
- Suggested starting cadence for a small agency:
  - Instagram: 3–5 Reels a week, plus Trial Reels for testing hooks.
  - YouTube: 2–4 Shorts a week, each linking to a related long-form video.
  - LinkedIn: 1–2 native videos a week from founder profiles, alternating with carousels.

### Gaps
- No large-sample India (IST) posting-time study was found for Reels, Shorts or LinkedIn.
- No India-specific engagement benchmarks by industry were found for 2025–2026.
- LinkedIn posting-time and frequency data for 2025–2026 was not retrieved.
