# Short-Form Video Playbook for Agencies, Freelancers and B2B/Service Businesses (Global + India, 2025–2026)

Research note, prepared 13 Oct 2026. Method: web search across vendor reports, platform guidance and trade press. Most direct page fetches failed (DNS errors on socialmediatoday.com, blog.sendspark.com, ppc.land, startupspells.com, support.google.com), so many findings come from search-result summaries of those pages and could not be checked line by line against the full text. Throughout, **[DATA]** marks platform or survey data with a stated sample, **[VENDOR]** marks figures a tool vendor reports about its own product, **[ANECDOTE]** marks single-case or self-reported claims, and **[OLD]** marks anything from before 2025.

---

## 1. Which agency/B2B accounts win on Reels, Shorts and LinkedIn, and which formats they use

### Takeaway
I found very little documented, verifiable evidence that names agency accounts and ties a specific format (roasts, teardowns, before/after redesigns, "I audited X") to client wins. The strongest documented case is a personal-brand coach using an educational Reel with a comment-keyword DM funnel. The teardown/roast format exists as a business model (Roast My Landing Page), but I found no published lead numbers for it. Platform data favours face-to-camera, vertical, human-led video, which supports founder-led formats more than polished brand reels.

### Cited Findings
- **[ANECDOTE] Ava Yuergens (Personal Brand Launch).** One educational Reel ("grow an Instagram from scratch") used ManyChat comment keywords to DM a link. It reportedly reached 7.9M views, about 13k leads and roughly 100–200 paying clients at $2k–$4k/month. This comes from a single blog write-up (2025), which I could not open or verify independently. — [Startup Spells: $2M Instagram Reel Playbook (2025)](https://startupspells.com/p/instagram-reel-playbook-viral-revenue/)
- **[ANECDOTE] Teardown/roast as a productised format.** Roast My Landing Page sells landing-page critiques and publishes video teardowns, such as "Video teardown: Chris Nguyen's sponsorship page." This shows the teardown format can work as both content and a product, but the site publishes no lead or conversion figures. — [Roast My Landing Page](https://www.roastmylandingpage.com/)
- **[DATA, platform-reported] LinkedIn formats.** A LinkedIn Creative Labs analysis, as reported by NetInfluencer, found face-to-camera videos had 34% higher engagement. Vertical formats had 34% longer dwell time than traditional square brand-awareness content. — [NetInfluencer: Top 5 factors driving B2B video engagement on LinkedIn](https://www.netinfluencer.com/top-5-factors-driving-b2b-video-engagement-on-linkedin/)
- **[DATA] LinkedIn video growth.** Total video viewership on LinkedIn grew 36% year over year (reported for fiscal Q1 2025). Weekly video creation by program members grew 67% YoY. LinkedIn began testing a vertical video feed in March 2024. Other outlets attribute the same 36% figure to calendar 2024 (154B views), so the time period is disputed. — [Digiday (2025)](https://digiday.com/marketing/linkedins-video-push-appears-to-be-working-in-2025/); [Teleprompter.com LinkedIn video stats](https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/linkedin-video-statistics)
- **[DATA] LinkedIn's bet on creator-led B2B video.** LinkedIn is scaling BrandLink, its program that pairs B2B advertisers with creator-led shows and publishers. BrandLink revenue reportedly grew nearly 200% quarter over quarter after the program was rebranded, and creator/publisher payouts more than tripled YoY. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/linkedin-bets-on-creator-trust-capture-b2b-video-dollars); [The Drum (Aug 2025)](https://www.thedrum.com/news/2025/08/26/why-linkedin-thinks-creator-led-shows-will-redefine-b2b-marketing)
- **[ANECDOTE] Web design education channels.** Ran Segall's Flux Academy channel (about 600k subscribers, 1.5k+ videos) runs "website redesign challenge" before/after videos. Chris Do's The Futur is a separate channel covering branding and business. I found no data linking either channel's Shorts to client acquisition. Both now mainly sell education rather than agency services. — [Flux Academy](https://www.flux-academy.com); [Flux Academy review](https://findawayabroad.com/post/flux-academy-review); [UX Planet](https://uxplanet.org/5-youtube-channels-that-can-help-you-keep-the-creative-juices-flowing-9d4b94706143)
- **[ANECDOTE, India] Indian agency results are mostly client-side and self-reported.** Thrive Future Works says a Reels strategy (consistent posting, hook-first scripts) helped skincare brand SSUNSU reach 400+ customers in a few months. WingComm says a Chennai D2C brand generated 8,000 leads in 5 months from Reels plus ads on a ₹50,000/month + 15% of ad spend model. Neither agency explains how it verified these figures. — [Thrive Future Works](https://thrivefutureworks.com/blog/instagram-reels-strategy-india); [WingComm](https://wingcomm.co/blogs/instagram-marketing-cost-india-2025.html)
- **[DATA, Meta-commissioned, India] Reels in the purchase funnel.** Meta-commissioned India research, as cited by Clouted, found Reels contributed to 81% of product discovery, 66% of consideration and 47% of purchase influence. The research was sponsored by the platform, so it is not neutral. — [Clouted: Instagram Reels marketing statistics](https://clouted.com/blog/instagram-reels-marketing-statistics)
- **[ANECDOTE, India] Schbang / Harshil Karia.** Schbang is one of India's largest independent agencies (founded 2015, offices in Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi-NCR, London and Amsterdam). Harshil Karia is a visible founder on LinkedIn, at conferences and on podcasts. In March 2025 he and Sohil Karia took full ownership of the agency. I found no source that analyses his content or links it to new-business wins. — [Manifest Media (Mar 2025)](https://www.manifest-media.in/digital/030325/schbang-strengthens-global-ambitions-as-harshil-and-sohil-karia-take-f.html); [e4m speaker profile](https://e4mevents.com/e4m-confluence-2024/speakers)

### Inferences
- The formats named in the brief all share three traits that platform data rewards: a person on camera, a specific named subject, and a visible transformation or verdict. The formats are teardowns/roasts, "I audited X's Instagram," before/after redesigns, client-result breakdowns and myth-busting. This is my inference from the LinkedIn Creative Labs findings and the Meta human-presence guidance in Section 4. I did not find a controlled comparison of these formats.
- In India, published "case studies" mostly show results an agency produced for clients (D2C, skincare). They rarely show how the agency won its own clients through short-form video. That evidence gap is itself an opportunity: an Indian agency that publicly documents its own pipeline from content would be unusual.

### Gaps
- I found no verifiable 2025–26 dataset or interview that names agency accounts (global or Indian) and gives leads or revenue from roast/teardown, "I audited X," or before/after formats. Known practitioners of these formats were not verified in this pass and should be checked before citing.
- I found no evidence on behind-the-scenes process content specifically for agencies.
- I could not access Indian agency founders' Instagram/LinkedIn metrics, because the platforms are not crawlable via search.

## 2. Founder-led vs brand-account content: reach and trust

### Takeaway
LinkedIn's own research and the data reported from it consistently favour identifiable people over company pages for trust and engagement. I found no controlled reach comparison between founder and brand accounts for agencies specifically.

### Cited Findings
- **[DATA] Video and trust.** In LinkedIn's 2025 B2B Marketing Benchmark (July 2025), marketers with a video strategy were 2.2x more likely to say their brand is well trusted. They were also 1.8x more likely to say it is well known. These are self-reported marketer perceptions, not buyer behaviour. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/b2b-marketing-is-starting-to-look-a-lot-like-b2c-and-its-working/552524/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/trust-becomes-b2bs-primary-growth-engine-as-video-reaches-78-adoption/)
- **[DATA, secondhand] Buyers prefer people.** An analysis attributes to LinkedIn guidance the finding that 67% of buyers prefer content from an identifiable person rather than a company. It also cites that 94% of B2B marketers say building trust is more important than ever. I could not trace either figure to the original LinkedIn document. — [SignalScout: Founder-led growth (Mar 2026)](https://signalscout.kokasexton.com/newsletter/founder-led-growth-trust-moat)
- **[DATA, secondhand] Creators on LinkedIn.** Reported LinkedIn research finds 59% of B2B buyers consume creator content on LinkedIn, more than on any other platform in the study. One source attributes this to 2025 research and another to a 2026 study. — [Startup Wired](https://startupwired.com/?p=13716); [Mediaweek: LinkedIn research on trusted voices](https://www.mediaweek.com.au/?p=248077)
- **[DATA, platform-reported] Face-to-camera.** Face-to-camera video gets 34% higher engagement on LinkedIn. — [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/top-5-factors-driving-b2b-video-engagement-on-linkedin/)
- **[OPINION] Authenticity over polish.** UGC, employee stories and real-world experiences outperform polished studio ads, and micro-niche community-building now beats chasing mass virality. — [EasyContent: Social media trends 2025 for agencies](https://easycontent.io/resources/social-media-trends-2025-agencies-clients)
- **[OPINION] LinkedIn guidance for founders.** — [RevBoss: What works on LinkedIn for B2B founders in 2025](https://revboss.com/blog/what-works-on-linkedin-for-b2b-founders-in-2025)

### Inferences
- For agencies and service businesses, where the buyer is effectively hiring the people, founder-led video likely does more for trust than brand-account video. A practical setup is for the founder's account to carry reach and trust while the brand account holds proof (case studies, portfolio).
- LinkedIn's money is moving toward creator-led B2B video through BrandLink and paid creator partnerships. That suggests the platform will keep distributing person-led video.

### Gaps
- I found no head-to-head reach data comparing founder and company-page posts from the LinkedIn B2B Institute or any independent study for 2025–26. The B2B Institute itself did not appear in results.
- I found no India-specific founder-versus-brand study.

## 3. How short-form video converts to leads for service businesses

### Takeaway
The most-documented conversion mechanics are comment-keyword DM automation and click-to-WhatsApp/message ads. Both turn high-intent commenters or clickers into a 1:1 conversation. The published conversion figures are almost all vendor case studies (ManyChat, Meta/WhatsApp), with very high opt-in rates among commenters. In India, Meta case studies show 12–31% lower cost per qualified lead when click-to-WhatsApp ads are optimised for leads.

### Cited Findings
- **[VENDOR] ManyChat / Jenna Kutcher.** Comment-triggered DMs drove an 85% webinar registration rate among commenters. Over 12 months the case reports 53,000 new email leads and $1.7M in sales. — [ManyChat case study via CaseStudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/manychat/case-study/jenna-kutcher-drives-17m-in-sales-with-manychat); [Blotato: ManyChat examples](https://www.blotato.com/blog/manychat-examples)
- **[VENDOR] ManyChat headline claim.** ManyChat claims a "700% lift in lead conversions" for client companies through Instagram automation. — [MarTech Series](https://martechseries.com/sales-marketing/messaging/manychat-drives-700-lift-in-lead-conversions-for-client-companies-via-instagram-automation-with-two-million-ig-messages-sent-daily/)
- **[ANECDOTE, unsourced] Opt-in rates.** One post claims comment-trigger opt-in rates of 60–80%. It gives no source. — [Skool post](https://www.skool.com/ai-income-court-3070/todays-case-using-manychat-to-build-an-ai-powered-lead-generation-machine)
- **[ANECDOTE] One Reel to clients.** The same Ava Yuergens case as Section 1: 7.9M views, about 13k leads and roughly 100–200 clients through a comment-keyword DM. The implied view-to-lead rate is about 0.16%. — [Startup Spells](https://startupspells.com/p/instagram-reel-playbook-viral-revenue/)
- **[VENDOR/Meta, India] Click-to-WhatsApp ads.** Nutryfit, an Indian nutrition platform, got more qualified leads at 12% lower cost per lead when it optimised click-to-WhatsApp ads for leads. Learnwise, a Kerala education-support platform, got 4x more qualified leads at 31% lower cost per qualified lead. Meta labels these results self-reported. — [WhatsApp Business: Nutryfit](https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/success-stories/nutryfit/); [WhatsApp Business: Learnwise](https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/success-stories/learnwise/)
- **[VENDOR] India click-to-WhatsApp costs.** AiSensy lists an average cost per lead of about ₹80, with no date or sample given. A benchmark blog says Meta tests showed moving from message optimisation to lead or purchase optimisation cut cost per lead or purchase by 12–79%. The same blog cites an India marketing-message cost of about ₹0.86 per message. — [AiSensy](https://aisensy.com/products/click-to-whatsapp-ads); [Spur: Click-to-WhatsApp benchmarks](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks); [Picky Assist](https://pickyassist.com/blog/whatsapp-advertising-in-india/)
- **[OPINION] Lead quality.** Cheaper leads from lead forms or WhatsApp often mean lower intent, so judge by cost per qualified lead and revenue, not raw cost per lead. — [echai.ventures](https://echai.ventures/startingup/paid-acquisition/should-i-try-whatsapp-or-lead-form-ads-instead-of-sending-people-to-a-website)
- **[DATA] Wistia State of Video 2025** (Mar 2025; 14M+ videos, 100k businesses, 1,300-person survey):
  - Over 40% of companies publish at least one video a week.
  - Only 28% spend more time promoting videos than making them, while 53% spend more time making them.
  - About 32% of contact-page visitors play videos there, and thank-you pages also perform well. This supports putting founder video on the conversion pages that short-form traffic lands on.
  — [Chief Marketer](https://chiefmarketer.com/wistia-state-of-video-report-engagement-down-content-under-promoted); [CMS Critic](https://cmscritic.com/lights-camera-ai-action-key-takeaways-from-wistias-2025-state-of-video-report); [Wistia report page](https://wistia.com/for/state-of-video-report)
- **[OPINION] Low-friction CTAs.** One guide recommends "DM me for a quote" or link-in-bio CTAs, and answering prospects' frequent questions in a series of 30–60 second videos. — [BlueFire Insurance: Lead gen with Reels & Shorts](https://www.bluefireinsurance.com/knowledge-center/independent-agent/social-media-lead-generation/)

### Inferences
- A plausible agency funnel based on the above: a short-form hook such as a teardown or "I audited X" leads to "comment AUDIT." The DM then gives a free mini-audit or checklist as a lead magnet, which leads to a booked call or a WhatsApp chat (in India). The video then sits on the booking or thank-you page.
- Opt-in rates among commenters look high (vendor claims of 60–85%). The bottleneck is the share of viewers who comment, and I found no benchmark for that.
- In India, WhatsApp should be the default conversation channel for service businesses. Meta's own Indian case studies are built around click-to-WhatsApp.

### Gaps
- I found no independent, agency-specific conversion data (views → comments → DMs → calls → clients).
- I found no benchmark for free-audit offer conversion rates.
- I found no published 2025–26 cost-per-lead benchmark for Indian B2B or agency services.

## 4. Paid short-form video ads for lead gen: Meta Reels and YouTube Shorts creative practices (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
Meta's guidance centres on 9:16, a hook in the first seconds, visible people, sound plus captions, and creative diversification (many distinct concepts), with Advantage+ creative generating variants automatically. Google's Demand Gen guidance applies the ABCD framework (Attention, Branding, Connection, Direction) to Shorts. It recommends repeating the CTA in voiceover and on screen, and mixing video with images. I found no lead-gen-specific Advantage+ benchmark.

### Cited Findings
- **[DATA, Meta via secondary sources] Creative traits.** 9:16 video, human presence, emotional storytelling and text overlays are linked to 16% lower CPA, 29% higher conversion rate and 11% more reach. Reels with expressive sound and visible people reportedly get 8% more conversions per dollar. About 80% of Reels plays are said to be sound-off, so captions matter. — [NoGood: Meta creative best practices](https://nogood.io/?p=44256); [Social Media Today: Meta tips on Reels hooks and creative diversification](https://socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-shares-tips-on-reels-hooks-creative-diversification-in-ads-and-threa/808182); [Cooler Insights: Meta creator event, Apr 2025](https://coolerinsights.com/2025/04/insights-from-metas-first-creator-event-in-singapore/)
- **[Platform guidance, secondhand] Advantage+ creative and diversification.** Advantage+ creative automatically generates variations (brightness, contrast, aspect ratio, text placement, auto-reframing to 9:16) and shifts delivery toward the winning versions. Meta favours a broad, diverse portfolio of creative concepts over endlessly refining a few ads. — [House of Marketers](https://houseofmarketers.com/meta-shares-tips-on-best-performing-ad-approaches-ai/); [True Future Media: Meta ads best practices 2025](https://www.truefuturemedia.com/articles/meta-ads-best-practices-2025); [Benly: Meta ad creative specs 2026](https://benly.ai/learn/ad-creative/meta-ads-creative-specs-2026)
- **[OPINION] Lead-gen funnel structure.** Use broad targeting with 3–5 creative variants at the top of the funnel. At the bottom, use offer-led creative that matches the landing page, and track cost per lead and conversion rate. — [True Future Media](https://www.truefuturemedia.com/articles/meta-ads-best-practices-2025)
- **[Platform, Google] Demand Gen (covers YouTube, Shorts, Discover and Gmail).** Follow ABCD and repeat the CTA in voiceover and on-screen graphics. Advertisers running video plus image ads get 6% more conversions per dollar than image-only advertisers. — [Google Ads Help: Creative Excellence Guide for Demand Gen](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14733311)
- **[VENDOR explainer] Shorts specifics.** Use fast, tight cuts, win the first five seconds, make vertical Shorts versions alongside long-form, and measure lift because last-click attribution undervalues video. — [Creatify: YouTube advertising best practices 2026](https://creatify.ai/blog/youtube-advertising-best-practices)
- **[DATA] AI in production.** Wistia 2025 reports a sharp rise in AI use in video production, and video budgets rising despite economic concerns. — [CMS Critic](https://cmscritic.com/lights-camera-ai-action-key-takeaways-from-wistias-2025-state-of-video-report); [Social Media Today: Wistia 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/wistia-brand-video-creation-trends-report-2025/743641)

### Inferences
- For agency lead-gen ads, a founder-on-camera, UGC-style 9:16 ad fits both Meta's human-presence guidance and Google's "Connection" pillar. Examples: a 15-second teardown of a recognisable brand's ad or site, or a "3 reasons your ads aren't converting" ad.
- Hook testing at volume (many distinct opening lines and concepts) fits Meta's push toward creative diversification. Advantage+ then handles surface-level variants such as size, text and brightness, not new concepts.
- In India, pairing Reels ads with a click-to-WhatsApp or lead-form destination is supported by Meta's Indian case studies (Section 3).

### Gaps
- I could not access Meta's primary creative documentation; the numbers above are secondary summaries of Meta briefings.
- I found no official Advantage+ or generative-AI creative lift data for lead-gen or B2B objectives.
- I found no YouTube Shorts ad benchmarks specific to B2B or services.

## 5. Personalised outreach videos (Loom/Sendspark/Vidyard and AI-generated): reply-rate data

### Takeaway
All of the reply-rate data is vendor-produced. Typical claims are 2–3x more replies than plain cold email, or 10–16% reply rates for video outreach against roughly 3–6% for cold email. Vidyard reports fast growth in AI-avatar adoption but published no reply-rate figure in what I could find. Treat all of these numbers as directional.

### Cited Findings
- **[VENDOR] Cold-email baseline (Sendspark).** B2B cold email reply rates reportedly fell from 6.8% to 5.8%. Legal services reply at 10%, consulting at 7.88% and healthcare at 7.49%. The years were stated ambiguously in the source. — [Sendspark: Cold email vs video email](https://blog.sendspark.com/cold-email-vs-video-email-replies)
- **[VENDOR] Sendspark lift claims.** Users report a 16% increase in open rates and a 26% increase in reply rates. Qwilr reported 3x replies. Sendspark also claims "20% to 800%" reply-rate lifts, a range too wide to use. — [Sendspark: Async video for B2B sales](https://blog.sendspark.com/async-video-b2b-sales-data-insights); [Sendspark: Video messages and response rates](https://blog.sendspark.com/video-messages-improve-customer-response-rates)
- **[VENDOR] Navikas case.** The headline metric was play rate, with 2–3x more email replies. — [Sendspark: Navikas case study](https://www.sendspark.com/case-study/navikas)
- **[VENDOR comparison] Typical ranges.** Video outreach typically gets 10–16% reply rates, and top performers exceed 25%. The comparison ranks Sendspark at 16–30% against a 3.43% average for cold email. — [SyncGTM: Personalized video email tools 2026](https://www.syncgtm.com/blog/personalized-video-emails-for-sales)
- **[DATA, user-base telemetry] Vidyard Video in Business Benchmark (AI Edition, covering 2024 activity).** Users created 943,305 videos, individual video creation rose 241%, and AI Avatar adoption rose 12x. In Feb 2025, Vidyard made its Video Sales Agent generally available; it sends AI-avatar personalised videos triggered from Salesforce, HubSpot, Salesloft and Marketo. — [Vidyard benchmark](https://www.vidyard.com/video-benchmark/); [Demand Gen Report](https://www.demandgenreport.com/industry-news/news-brief/vidyard-automates-the-creation-and-delivery-of-personalized-sales-videos/48840)
- **[OLD, 2021] Vidyard with Demand Metric.** Over 70% of reps using custom-recorded video reported higher prospect engagement. — [BusinessWire (Sept 2021)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210922005143/en/Vidyard-Report-Reveals-Sales-Teams-Using-Video-Have-Increased-Response-Rates-Reduced-Deal-Cycles)

### Inferences
- For agencies, a personalised teardown video (for example, a 60–90 second Loom of the prospect's own website or Instagram) combines outreach and proof of skill. It is the 1:1 version of the public "I audited X" format, and vendor data suggests it roughly doubles or triples replies over text-only email.
- AI-avatar videos scale volume, but I found no data comparing their reply rates with real recorded video. A risk to consider is that authenticity is the main reason the format works.

### Gaps
- I found no independent, peer-reviewed or audited reply-rate benchmark for 2025–26.
- I found no data on AI-avatar versus human-recorded reply rates.
- I found no India-specific video-prospecting data (e.g., video sent over WhatsApp versus email).

## 6. Pitfalls: what agency content flops

### Takeaway
I found no rigorous 2025–26 study of why agency short-form video fails. The available guidance is opinion: chasing mass virality instead of niche relevance, over-polished brand content, no content pillars tied to the buyer, and too little promotion or distribution. One measurement pitfall is well-supported: judging success by views rather than pipeline.

### Cited Findings
- **[OPINION, 2025] Virality vs niche, polish vs authenticity.** Micro-niche audience-building beats chasing mass virality, and UGC or employee stories beat polished studio ads. — [EasyContent](https://easycontent.io/resources/social-media-trends-2025-agencies-clients)
- **[DATA] Under-promotion.** Only 28% of teams spend more time promoting videos than making them, against 53% who do the reverse (Wistia 2025). — [Chief Marketer](https://chiefmarketer.com/wistia-state-of-video-report-engagement-down-content-under-promoted)
- **[OPINION] Vanity metrics.** A performance-oriented agency ties video to business outcomes rather than views or reach. — [Fresh Content Society](https://freshcontentsociety.com/short-form-video-agency-how-brands-create-content/)
- **[OPINION] Low conversion from keyword offers.** A real-estate practitioner reported that a comment-keyword offer worked but drew few comments. This suggests the CTA alone does not fix weak offer-audience fit. — [Sprout Social community](https://community.sproutsocial.com/questions/479)
- **[OPINION, OLD 2023] No content pillars.** Without core content pillars tied to buyer interests, content falls flat. — [Yarnit](https://www.yarnit.app/blog/avoid-these-common-content-creation-mistakes)
- **[OPINION] Agencies fail on basics.** — [Vista Social](https://vistasocial.com/insights/social-media-marketing-agencies-fail/)
- **[Caution] Unreliable proof.** Several "47 qualified leads from Reels"-type agency claims appear only on Framer template or sales pages and should not be treated as evidence. — [Rankup Framer template](https://rankup-content-agency.framer.website/)

### Inferences
- Likely flops, based on the above:
  - Generic "marketing tips" that peers like but buyers ignore. The audience ends up full of other marketers, not prospects.
  - Polished agency showreels with no person and no specific subject.
  - Roasts that punch down at small businesses, which carry reputational risk.
  - Viral reach outside the target geography or niche. For Indian agencies selling locally, a global audience may not convert.
  - Content with no conversion path, such as no keyword DM, WhatsApp link or offer.
- These are reasoned inferences, not measured findings.

### Gaps
- I found no data on failure rates or negative-reach effects of roast content.
- I found no study of audience mismatch, where an agency attracts peers instead of buyers.
- I found no evidence from Indian agencies on what failed.
